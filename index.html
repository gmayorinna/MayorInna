Social computing · AI governance · Digital strategy

Hi, I'm May-er-in-na Gu-rung

I work at the intersection of full-stack engineering, data systems, and development outcomes. At the World Bank Group's Department of Outcomes, I lead the technical development of the ABPE platform, connecting front-end interfaces, back-end application logic, and data processing workflows into a cohesive analytical environment. Working with regional leads covering 152 countries, I translate complex operational requirements into platform capabilities that make country and regional data easier to navigate, analyze, and use in decision-making.

My research explores the computational and social dynamics of information systems, with interests in social computing, narrative diffusion, misinformation, and responsible AI. I combine computational modeling with social theory to examine how information propagates through networks, how algorithmic bias shapes user experiences, and how digital platforms influence public discourse. Through persona-based narrative simulation, I study how identity and social context affect interactions with information systems, connecting research insights to the design and evaluation of AI-enabled technologies.
